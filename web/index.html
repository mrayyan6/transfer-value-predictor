        Model against market

        Each dot is a player, predicted by a model that never saw them during training. On the dotted line the two agree exactly. Log scales, so a €5m miss on a €10m player counts the same as a €50m miss on a €100m one. Click a dot to open the player.

        What it can't see

        • Contract length. A player with a year left is worth far less to sell, and the model has no idea.
        • Injuries, reputation, shirt sales, international football, the agent. All of that is in the market price and none of it is in the stats.
        • Keepers. Goals and tackles say nothing about a goalkeeper, so they're left out entirely.
        • Age does most of the work. Young players are priced on what they might become, which no season of stats can show.

        Stats from Sofascore, market values from transfermarkt-datasets. Outfield players with 600+ league minutes.
